Is high fructose corn syrup low FODMAP?

No, it is high FODMAP.

High fructose corn syrup is high FODMAP, because of its fructose. Instead: Maple syrup or plain sugar.

Why high fructose corn syrup causes symptoms

fructose are short-chain carbohydrates that the small intestine absorbs poorly. They pull water in, then ferment in the colon — which is where the gas, bloating and cramping come from. The reaction is a matter of dose, and it does not always arrive the same day.

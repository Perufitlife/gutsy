Find the foods behind your gut symptoms

Tumzy is a private food & symptom diary for people living with IBS, bloating, and food sensitivities. Log what you eat and how your gut feels — Tumzy connects the dots, even when a reaction lands 24–72 hours later, the timing simple diaries miss.

  • Find your triggers. Tumzy ranks the foods most often followed by your symptoms, and shows the foods that look safe for you.
  • Private by design. Your food and symptom logs stay on your device. No account, no cloud, no one reading them.
  • Honest. Tumzy is a wellness journal to help you spot patterns — it is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always talk to a clinician about your health.

Tumzy+ unlocks your full ranked trigger list, safe foods, typical reaction timing, and weekly trends — all computed privately on your phone.
